Build a University Lab & Equipment Booking System with a modern SaaS aesthetic similar to Linear and Vercel: neutral grays, one accent color, Inter or Geist font, rounded cards, subtle borders, light and dark mode. Admin analytics should follow Stripe/Vercel dashboards: KPI cards, utilization heatmap, peak-hour chart, most-booked equipment. Use shadcn/ui and Tremor/Recharts for components. Roles: student, faculty, lab staff, admin.
